# Sources: lost in a parking garage (23 Nov 2026)

Everyday-emergencies queue, topic 4. Owner's hook: "The same car has passed you three times."

| Claim in the reel | Source |
|---|---|
| If you think you're being followed, go to a populated place (lift lobby / exit booth, lights, people, a guard) | Houston Police: "If you think you are being followed... Go to a safe place such as a police station, fire station, business or other populated area." https://www.houstontx.gov/police/robbery/vehicle.htm ; Roseville: "ask security personnel or another person to walk with you" and "If you observe any suspicious activities or persons in the area, exit the lot immediately." https://roseville.ca.us/news/what_s_happening_in_roseville/parking_lot_and_parking_garage_safety_tips |
| Your phone may have saved your parking spot by itself | Apple Maps "automatically drops a parked car marker on its map when you exit the vehicle" (CarPlay or Bluetooth), AARP https://www.aarp.org/auto/trends-technology/ways-to-find-lost-car/ ; Google Maps (iPhone): "Maps automatically saves your parking location" via Bluetooth/CarPlay/USB, https://support.google.com/maps/answer/7257797 . Reel says "may" because it depends on phone and pairing. |
| Panic button makes the car honk/flash so you can locate it | SlashGear: "if you forget where you parked your car, you can press the panic button to help you locate it" https://www.slashgear.com/1803259/car-key-fob-panic-button-uses/ ; AARP: "Many vehicles will flash their lights, honk or otherwise respond when you press the lock or panic button." ; Roseville: "Find the panic button on your car's remote device and learn how to use it." |
| Keys in hand before reaching the car | Florida AG: "Approach your vehicle with your keys already in your hand." https://www.myfloridalegal.com/node/1070 ; LSU Police https://lsu.edu/police/safety/automobile.php |
| Glance at the back seat before getting in | Florida AG: "Be sure to look in the back seat." ; Nova Scotia: "take a quick look in the back seat" https://novascotia.ca/just/prevention/tips_auto_safety.asp |
| Lock the doors as soon as you get in | LSU Police: "Close and lock your doors as soon as you get into your vehicle." |

Dropped: "hold the key fob to your chin to boost range". Siemens (simulation) and Snopes say it works, but Keysight's measurement says it does not increase range, and no source gives a measured figure. Not used.
Jokes ("hunting for parking", "honks like it's stuck in Delhi traffic") are humour, not factual claims.
