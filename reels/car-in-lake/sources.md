# Sources: What if your car rolled into a lake?

Checked 2026-11-17. Each claim the reel states, with where it was verified.

| Claim in the reel | Sources |
| --- | --- |
| The car floats for **about** a minute, and that is the time to get out. | JEMS (Giesbrecht's tests: floats 15 to 63 s before water reaches the bottom of the side windows); KidsAndCars ("roughly 60 seconds before escape becomes nearly impossible"); Lifesaving Society (30 s to several minutes). Stated as "about a minute". |
| Don't call first; get out first, then call. | JEMS (Giesbrecht: calling 9-1-1 "steals precious seconds"); Lifesaving Society ("it is best to escape first"). |
| Waiting for the car to fill up and then opening the door is a myth. | AAA Club Alliance ("this is deadly advice"); JEMS (Giesbrecht: "The wrong thing was taught"); Lifesaving Society (by the time pressure equalises "it is typically too late"). |
| Water pressure jams the door. | Lifesaving Society ("extremely difficult or impossible to open the driver's side and passenger doors"); KidsAndCars ("The door is not your exit. The window is."). |
| Four steps: Seatbelt off, Window open, Children out (oldest first), Out. | JEMS (protocol Giesbrecht helped write: "Seatbelts; Windows; Children...; Out, with children exiting first from the oldest to the youngest"); KidsAndCars ("oldest to youngest"); Lifesaving Society (Seatbelts, Windows, Children, Go). |
| Open the window right away; power windows usually still work at first. | AAA Club Alliance (power windows "continue to work for up to a minute"); KidsAndCars (up to about a minute); JEMS (30 to 60 s). |
| If stuck, smash a side window near the corner. | KidsAndCars ("break a side window with a dedicated escape tool, aim near a corner"); AAA Club Alliance ("break glass in a window's corner where it is structurally weakest"). Windshields are laminated and won't break (AAA ACG), so the reel only shows a side window. |
| Keep a window breaker within reach (not stored away). | KidsAndCars (visor, keychain or centre console, "Not the glove compartment"); AAA Club Alliance; AAA ACG ("keep securely in reach"). |
| In India, call 112. | Single emergency number 112 under the Emergency Response Support System (ERSS): Ministry of Home Affairs ERSS page; Business Standard "Dial 112 for help in any emergency"; Factly explainer on ERSS/112. |

Not stated in the reel on purpose: death counts (sources disagree: about 400 a year in North America vs 400 to 600 in the US), and laminated side windows (true for some newer cars, but too long for the reel).
