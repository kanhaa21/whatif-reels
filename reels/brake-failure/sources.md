# Sources: What if your brakes fail at 100 km/h?

- Handbrake: pull it gradually, don't yank it, or the rear wheels can lock and spin: https://www.globalvillagespace.com/tech/what-to-do-if-your-brakes-fail-at-highway-speed
- Rubbing against a curb or guardrail to slow down: https://www.globalvillagespace.com/tech/what-to-do-if-your-brakes-fail-at-highway-speed
- Don't switch off the engine: https://uscargocontrol.com/blogs/blog/what-should-i-do-if-my-brakes-fail
