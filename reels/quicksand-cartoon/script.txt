One wrong step, and the ground grabs your legs.
You're on a riverbank, alone, and the sand is turning to soup.
You panic and pull. Your foot won't budge.
Pull harder? You'd need the strength to lift a car.
Every kick sinks you deeper. The movies say you vanish.
But here's the secret: quicksand is about twice as dense as your body.
You can only sink to about your waist.
So stop pulling. Wiggle your legs slowly, lean back, and float out.
Quicksand can't swallow you. Panic can.
